Console GTA RP, answered

How do I join a GTA 5 RP server on PS5 or Xbox?

Console roleplay happens in invite only GTA Online sessions organised through Discord. Pick a server here, press Join, read the rules channel and follow the steps to get verified. Most servers then invite you to their session from the in game invite list during scheduled patrols.

Do I need mods or a PC?

No. Console RP uses the normal GTA Online game on PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S or Xbox One. Everything extra, like the police database, 911 calls and dispatch, runs through Discord and the RolePlayManager web CAD.

How are servers ranked?

Top voted counts votes from the last 30 days. Anyone with a Discord account older than 14 days can vote once every 12 hours for each server. Premium servers are listed above free servers, and featured servers appear at the top.

What happens when GTA 6 comes out?

Console roleplay runs in GTA Online, so it stays on GTA 5 until GTA VI has an online mode to roleplay in. When it does, the servers listed here can move over, and the directory will list GTA VI servers too.
