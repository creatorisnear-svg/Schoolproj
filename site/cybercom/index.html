RPM CyberCom

A bot in every voice channel

Officers call traffic stops and 10-80s on the radio, and the bot moves everyone where they need to be. Civilians move by voice too. Voice moderation flags real problems to your staff, while roleplay stays in the game.

$9.99 a month, with or without Premium. Includes the AI voice dispatcher.
Get RPM CyberCom Add the bot free

On the police radio

Officers start with "Dispatch", the same as with the AI dispatcher. The bot answers on the radio and does the moving.

Traffic stops by voice

The bot moves you to a free traffic stop channel, and the helper in the driver's channel asks if they want to be moved in.

"Dispatch, show me on a 10-11 with Blade."

Plates and names in the stop

Run plates and names from inside the traffic stop, without going back to the patrol channel.

"Dispatch, run plate ABC123."

10-80s reach every unit

The pursuit goes out on the police radio, and any unit can ask to be moved straight in.

"Dispatch, attach me to the 10-80."

Everyone goes back

Clear the stop and everyone in it is moved back to the channel they came from. Anyone who wants to stay just says no.

"Dispatch, show me off my 10-11."

Your CAD keeps up

Every 10-11, 10-80 and 10-8 called by voice updates the officer's status in your CAD, so the status board is always right.

The AI dispatcher, included

Your patrol channels get the full AI voice dispatcher: 911 calls read out on the radio and officers answered by voice, with or without Premium.

For civilians

In civilian channels the wake word is "RPM".

Move by voice

Join a friend without waiting for staff to drag you.

"RPM, move me to Mia."

Asked, never dragged

When an officer pulls someone over, the bot asks before moving them, and they answer yes or no without a wake word.

"Blade, would you like to be moved into the 10-11 channel?"

Voice moderation

Everything said in the channels RPM CyberCom covers is transcribed, so your staff hear about real problems even when nobody reports them.

Real problems get flagged

Slurs, real life threats, possible self harm and other Discord rule breaks are posted to your flag channel with who said it and where. Threat and self harm flags ping the staff roles you choose.

Roleplay is never flagged

Threats and self harm are checked by AI first, so robberies, shootouts and threats between characters stay in the game.

Your own words and phrases

Add the words and phrases your rules ban, and they are flagged as soon as they are said.

Transcripts for your staff

Staff read what was said with /voicemoderation, by channel, day and member. Every flag can show the three minutes around it. Transcripts are kept 3, 7 or 14 days.

Set it up in a few minutes

No hosting and nothing to install in the game.

  1. Get RPM CyberCom for your server on the pricing page.
  2. In your server, run /config cybercom and pick your police radio, traffic stop and civilian channels.
  3. Add one helper bot for each traffic stop and civilian channel. It walks you through them one at a time, with your server already picked, and moves on by itself as each one joins.
  4. Run /config voicemoderation to choose where flags go, who can read transcripts and how long they are kept.

Questions

Do I need Premium?

No. RPM CyberCom is $9.99 a month on its own and includes the AI voice dispatcher on your police radio. If your server also has Premium, both keep working together.

Does it work for PS5 and Xbox servers?

Yes. Everything happens in your Discord voice channels, so it works the same whichever console your members play on.

Do members know the channels are transcribed?

Yes. In every channel it covers, the helper bot's name ends in (transcribing), and when greetings are on it tells people who join how to talk to it and that the channel is transcribed. See the privacy policy.

Who can read the transcripts?

Your staff: admins and people added with /staff, or the roles you pick in /config voicemoderation.

How long are transcripts kept?

14 days unless you choose 3 or 7 in /config voicemoderation. After that they are deleted.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Questions first? Ask in the support server.
